## App name candidates

You asked me to propose five and pick the winner. **MERIDIAN is used as the
working name throughout this build** — swap in your choice and the only files
that change are `app.config.ts`, `package.json`, and the store listing.

### 1 · MERIDIAN ← *working name*

A meridian is both a line of longitude and, in astronomy, **the highest point a
body reaches in its arc**. It is a navigation instrument and an apex in one word,
which is precisely the product. It also sits naturally alongside SOLAR and
ECLIPSE at the top of the ladder — the celestial vocabulary is already there.

*Genre-cliché check:* clean. No fitness or RPG app in the category uses it. Reads
as an instrument, not a game.
*Icon at 48px:* a single arc with a crossing vertical — legible at any size.

### 2 · THRESHOLD

The measured barrier you cross each session. Industrial, ownable, and it carries
the verification idea implicitly — a threshold is something you physically pass
through, which is what a gym check-in is.

*Genre-cliché check:* clean, though "Threshold" is a common word in training
science (lactate threshold), which is either a resonance or a confusion.
*Icon at 48px:* a doorway aperture. Strong silhouette, slightly generic.

### 3 · PROVING

From "proving ground". The verification moat stated in one word — a rank here is
*proven*, not claimed. The most on-message of the five.

*Genre-cliché check:* clean. Slight risk of reading as a verb fragment.
*Icon at 48px:* a stamp or seal mark. Distinctive, but closer to a
certification-app aesthetic than a competitive one.

### 4 · KILN

Where raw material is hardened by sustained heat. Short, hard consonants, entirely
ownable, and no existing product in the category is near it. The most *memorable*
of the five.

*Genre-cliché check:* completely clean.
*Icon at 48px:* excellent — a simple aperture form. The concern is that the
metaphor needs a beat to land, and store listings do not get a beat.

### 5 · VANTAGE

Height earned, plus the view from it. Pairs well with a ladder product and reads
as achievement rather than exertion.

*Genre-cliché check:* mostly clean, but "Vantage" appears in several fitness and
finance products. Weakest trademark position of the five.
*Icon at 48px:* stacked horizontal marks of increasing width.

**Recommendation: MERIDIAN**, with KILN as the alternative if you want the more
distinctive and less descriptive option.

## App icon concept

**Must read at 48px and be distinguishable from every competitor.**

A single **arc crossed by a vertical rule** — the meridian mark itself, rendered
as an instrument etching.

| Property | Decision |
|---|---|
| Background | `#0A0B0D` near-black |
| Mark | `#00E0B8` signal, 2px stroke at 48px |
| Composition | Arc occupying the lower two-thirds, vertical rule bisecting |
| Corners | System-standard, no custom shape |

**What it deliberately avoids:** no gradient, no dumbbell, no flame, no lightning
bolt, no letter mark, no glowing blue. Every competitor in the category uses at
least one of those. A thin cyan geometric etching on near-black will not be
confused with any of them in a search result, which is the only test that matters
at 48px.

### FAQ

**Do I need a gym?**
No. Calibration, logging, quests, raids, rivals, guilds and the ladder up to
COBALT all work with bodyweight training anywhere. A partner gym is only needed to
climb past COBALT.

**What if my gym isn't a partner?**
Your sessions log normally and everything works — they just do not carry a
verified badge. You can nominate your gym for the partner programme from the app.

**Is this a diet app?**
No, and it never will be. There is no calorie tracking, no weight targets and no
body-composition anything anywhere in the product. Meridian measures performance.

**What happens if I get injured?**
Log it. Your streak pauses indefinitely, your rank freezes, nothing decays, and
all challenge prompts stop. There is no penalty and there is no guilt copy.

**What happens if I stop training?**
Nothing for 21 days. After that you lose 1 point of power score per day, and it
floors one tier below your career best. You cannot fall further than that, ever.

**Can I buy my way up?**
No. See proof section 3.

**How old do I have to be?**
16.

## First-week retention plan

The design goal from §2 FAILURE 3: **a standing obligation to another human being
by the end of week one.**

### Day 0 — install

| Beat | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Rank exists before the account does | §4 cold open. The user has something to lose at signup. |
| One quest already active, expiring in 24 h | §4 step 6 |
| Rank card generated and shareable | §13 |
| The cap is named | If capped, the user is told exactly what tier is being withheld and how to unlock it |

### Day 1 — the second session

| Beat | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Notification (1 of 2 allowed) | AXIOM names the weakest pillar and today's primary quest |
| Streak starts at 2 | The §16 copy is encouraging only |
| First pillar movement is visible | Post-session summary shows exactly what moved and why |

**Risk:** a first session that scores less than the user expected. Mitigated by
the summary explaining the arithmetic rather than showing a bare number.

### Day 3 — the social hook

| Beat | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Rival suggestions surface | §11 auto-suggest, weighted to same venue then same city |
| Rest day handled well | If day 3 is a declared rest day, the board shows the rest philosophy — not an empty screen or a guilt prompt |
| Weekly quest at ~40% | Visible cumulative progress |

**This is the critical day.** An operator who accepts a rival on day 3 has a
weekly appointment with a person. One who does not is on a solo grind and will
churn on day 12, exactly as §2 predicts.

### Day 7 — the first re-evaluation

| Beat | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Weekly tier re-evaluation runs | Requires ≥1 verified session, which converts venue check-ins |
| First rival week resolves | Win, loss or draw — all three generate a reason to open the app |
| Streak freeze explained | Proactively, before it is needed, so it reads as generosity not rescue |
| Weekly quest completes | 500 XP, likely a level-up |
| First anomaly plausible | IRON WEEK if every scheduled session was hit |

### Day 14 — the commitment

| Beat | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| First raid invite | From a rival or a guild member; raids need a party, so the invite is a real ask |
| Territory becomes visible | The operator's venue position in Bharatpur |
| 7-day premium trial offered | Only now, after AXIOM's text coaching has demonstrated value |
| Second rival slot suggested | Widening the obligation network |
